# Privacy Policy - BeraniBicara App

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Introduction

BeraniBicara ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Name and email address (for account creation)
- School class information
- Profile pictures (optional)

### 2.2 Report Information
- Incident reports and descriptions
- Photos/videos uploaded as evidence
- Location data (if provided)
- Timestamps of reports

### 2.3 Technical Information
- Device information and identifiers
- App usage analytics
- Push notification tokens

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain our service
- Process and respond to incident reports
- Send notifications about report status
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Ensure platform safety and security

## 4. Information Sharing

We DO NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only:
- With school TPPK team members (for report handling)
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect rights, property, or safety

## 5. Anonymous Reporting

For anonymous reports:
- We do not store personally identifiable information
- Reports are processed without linking to user accounts
- IP addresses and device identifiers are not retained

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:
- Encryption of sensitive data
- Secure data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits

## 7. Data Retention

- Account information: Retained while account is active
- Reports: Retained as per school policy requirements
- Anonymous reports: Processed and anonymized permanently

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for school use. We:
- Obtain appropriate consent for users under 13
- Limit data collection to necessary information only
- Provide parental access rights upon request

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Withdraw consent for data processing

## 10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Supabase**: Database and authentication
- **Firebase**: Push notifications
- **Google Sign-In**: Authentication option

Each service has its own privacy policy.

We may update this policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through the app or email.
